How to Play

The objective of 87-0 is to build a Counter-Strike roster capable of creating an era. Your success is determined by a simulation that evaluates the combined impact of leadership, firepower, support play, and consistency across five players drawn from the greatest Major teams in Counter-Strike history.

1. Building Your Roster

To finalize a lineup and trigger the simulation, you must draft exactly five players while filling the following roles:

  • IGL (In-Game Leader)
  • AWPer
  • Support
  • Rifler 1
  • Rifler 2

Each role can only be filled once.

Many players can play multiple roles. For example:

  • ZywOo can be drafted as either an AWPer or Rifler
  • FalleN can be drafted as either an IGL or AWPer
  • Jame can be drafted as either an IGL or AWPer

Choose carefully, as role assignments can dramatically change your final score.

Important: A player may only be drafted once. Different versions of the same player cannot appear on the same roster.

Examples:

  • ❌ s1mple (2016) + s1mple (2021)
  • ❌ apEX (2015) + apEX (2025)
  • ✅ GuardiaN (2016) + s1mple (2021)

2. The Draft Pool

The draft pool consists primarily of teams that achieved one of the following:

  • Won a Counter-Strike Major
  • Reached a Major Final
  • Reached a Major Semifinal
  • Special historical outlier teams

Each draft round presents a random historical roster from which you may select one player.

Because teams are drawn from different eras, you may create lineups that never existed in reality.

Examples:

  • donk + ZywOo
  • NiKo + s1mple
  • karrigan + FalleN
  • device + ropz

The goal is not realism, it is creating the strongest possible era team.

3. How Era Score Is Calculated

Once your team is complete, the simulation evaluates four core categories:

Category Purpose
Firepower Individual fragging ability and carry potential
IGL Leadership, strategy, and tactical structure
Support Utility, teamwork, and enabling teammates
Consistency Ability to perform at a high level over time

The simulation combines these categories into a single Era Score.

Firepower

Firepower is heavily influenced by:

  • Your AWPer
  • Both Riflers

Elite stars such as:

  • s1mple
  • ZywOo
  • donk
  • NiKo
  • device

can significantly raise a team's ceiling.

IGL

Only the player drafted into the IGL slot contributes to the team's IGL score.

Examples of elite IGLs include:

  • gla1ve
  • karrigan
  • FalleN
  • apEX
  • Zeus

A great IGL can elevate a strong roster into an era-defining one.

Support

Only the player drafted into the Support slot contributes to the team's Support score.

Elite support players often provide less raw firepower but contribute heavily to team success.

Examples include:

  • Xyp9x
  • Perfecto
  • KRIMZ
  • TACO
  • sjuush

Consistency

Consistency measures how reliably a player performs across events and throughout an era.

A team of volatile stars may have tremendous upside but can struggle to achieve the same score as a roster built around sustained excellence.

4. Team Construction Strategy

Building an era team is not simply about drafting the five highest-rated players available.

Successful teams usually balance:

  • Elite star power
  • Strong leadership
  • Reliable support play
  • Consistent performers

Examples of historical era rosters include:

  • Astralis (2018–2019)
  • NAVI (2021)
  • Vitality (2023–2025)

The most successful drafts often combine a world-class AWPer, a legendary IGL, and multiple elite riflers.

5. Understanding Results

After simulation, your roster receives an Era Score and a classification.

Era Score Result
95+ Generational Era
90-94 Back-to-Back Majors
80-89 Major Champions
70-79 Fluke Run
60-69 Playoff Merchants
Below 60 Disband Immediately

Only the strongest combinations of stars, leaders, and support players will consistently reach the highest tiers.

Not every lineup containing famous names will become an era.

Sometimes the difference between a Major champion and a generational dynasty comes down to a single role.

6. Experiment and Discover

There is no single correct draft strategy.

Try:

  • Building around the greatest AWPers
  • Creating superteams of star riflers
  • Pairing legendary IGLs with modern firepower
  • Recreating historical Major-winning cores
  • Constructing impossible dream rosters from multiple eras

The challenge of 87-0 is discovering which combinations of Counter-Strike legends can truly create an era.
